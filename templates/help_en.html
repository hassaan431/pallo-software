1. Introducing a New Product or Brand

Suppliers & Brands

Before receiving shipments or listing products in the catalog, the system must register the supplying partner and corresponding brand name.

1
Register the Supplier & Brand: Navigate to Suppliers & Brands, click "Add Supplier / Brand", enter supplier details (Name, Contact, Phone), and define their brand label.
2
Register the Catalog Item: Navigate to Inventory, click "Add New Product", specify product name, cost price, wholesale/selling price, brand, and packaging unit.
Pro Tip: Newly added products initialize with zero active stock. Physical inventory increments automatically when inbound purchase shipments are logged.

2. Receiving New Inventory (Inbound Purchases)

Purchases Workstation

When physical shipments arrive at warehouse facilities, recording an inbound purchase immediately replenishes inventory and updates accounting cost baselines.

1
Log Shipment Receipt: Navigate to Purchases and click "New Purchase".
2
Select Warehouse & Vendor: Designate the receiving storage facility and supplier.
3
Add Line Items: Search items by name or SKU, enter exact received carton/unit quantities and unit costs. Add any applicable freight or tax surcharges.
4
Confirm Inbound: Submit to update real-time stock balances across warehouse ledgers.

3. Recording a Sale & Generating Invoices

Sales Workstation

Sales transactions simultaneously deduct stock from dispatch warehouses, compute gross profit margins, and generate PDF invoices.

1
Initiate Invoice: Navigate to Sales and click "Record Sale" (or open the POS Terminal).
2
Customer & Location: Pick the customer account and the dispatching warehouse facility.
3
Add Products & Terms: Select line items, set applicable discounts, record upfront payment, and choose payment terms (Cash, Cheque, or Credit).
4
Generate PDF: Click "Print Invoice PDF" to generate an itemized invoice complete with paid stamp or outstanding balance.

4. Transferring Stock Between Storage Facilities

Transfers Workstation

Inter-warehouse stock movements balance inventory across regional storage locations without affecting sales revenue or cost ledgers.

1
Navigate to Transfers and click "New Transfer".
2
Designate Source Warehouse and Destination Warehouse.
3
Specify transfer quantities per item, add transport notes, and submit transfer verification.
