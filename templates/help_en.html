📘 AK Distributors — Help Center

Welcome to the AK Distributors Inventory and Invoice Management System. This comprehensive guide is designed to help you and your team navigate the software, manage your daily operations, and streamline your business workflows.

📑 Table of Contents

  1. General Workflows (Step-by-Step)

General Workflows

1. Introducing a New Product or Brand 📦

Before a new product can be added to your catalogue or received in a shipment, the system needs to recognize the Supplier/Brand.

Step 1: Register the Supplier/Brand
Navigate to: Suppliers & Brands

  1. Click the Add Supplier/Brand button.
  2. Fill in the required details: Supplier Name, Primary Contact Information, and the Brand Name they provide.
  3. Save the entry. This links the brand to your supplier permanently.

Step 2: Add the Product to the Catalogue
Navigate to: Inventory

  1. Click the Add New Product button.
  2. Complete the product profile (Name, Description, Pricing, and the Brand you created in Step 1).
  3. Save the product. (Note: It will have 0 stock until you record a purchase shipment.)

2. Receiving New Inventory (Purchases) 📥

When a physical shipment of goods arrives at your warehouse, you must record it as a Purchase.

Step 1: Log the Incoming Shipment
Navigate to: Purchases

  1. Click New Purchase.
  2. Select the Supplier and the Destination Warehouse.
  3. Search for the products received, and input the exact quantities and unit costs.
  4. Add any freight, tax, or shipping fees.
  5. Submit the purchase to instantly increase your stock levels.

3. Recording a Sale & Invoicing 🧾

Recording a sale deducts from your active inventory, calculates your profit margins, and generates a professional invoice.

Step 1: Process the Sale
Navigate to: Sales

  1. Click Record Sale.
  2. Select the Customer and the Dispatching Warehouse.
  3. Add products, adjust quantities, and set the final selling price (you can offer line-item discounts here).
  4. Add applicable Taxes/Delivery Fees, and mark the Payment Status (Paid, Partial, Unpaid).
  5. Submit the sale and click Generate PDF Invoice.

4. Transferring Stock Between Warehouses 🚚

If you operate multiple storage locations, you will frequently need to move inventory between them.

Navigate to: Transfers

  1. Click New Transfer.
  2. Select the Source Warehouse and Destination Warehouse.
  3. Add the specific products and quantities, then Confirm.

Module Guide

  • Dashboard & Reports: Financial command center. View live snapshots of Sales, Gross Profit, Net Profit, and generate custom financial reports.
  • Products (Inventory & Purchases): Master list of all products, historical shipments, and internal stock movements.
  • Suppliers & Brands: CRM for your vendors and physical storage network manager (Warehouses).
  • Sales & Customers: Master ledger of all transactions, invoices, and your client directory.
  • Administration (Expenses & Users): Log business expenses (crucial for accurate Net Profit) and manage staff access.
  • Brochure Settings: Control the content of your public-facing product catalogue website.
  • POS Catalogue: Streamlined Point-of-Sale interface for rapid transactions.
